read `MANIFESTO.md` for voice reference. read `references/counter-writing.md` for voice rules.

voice rules. enforce strictly.

- lowercase by default.
- ALL CAPS for emphasis bursts only. one to three words at a time.
- fragments. so are imperatives. so are sentences.
- periods where they don't belong.
- no em-dashes. no "—". use line breaks instead.
- no ellipses except trailing off menacingly.
- no emoji. ever.
- no exclamation marks except in declared parody.
- no semicolons. write two sentences.

banned phrases. check `references/counter-writing.md` for the full list. partial list:
- Get started
- Learn more
- Discover
- Explore
- Sign up free
- Trusted by
- Delightful, Beautiful, Powerful, Seamless, Effortless, Modern
- Loved by, Join thousands of, From idea to launch

structures to reach for. mix freely.

1. THE REFUSAL. "we will not. nobody asked."
2. THE DECLARATION. "THIS IS THE FIRE."
3. THE FRAGMENT. "color. clash. crowd."
4. THE ENEMY CALLOUT. "they want X. we don't."
5. THE FIVE-LAW LIST. numbered laws, terse, opinionated.

deliver.

- the requested copy in voice.
- if multiple sections needed (manifesto block, tagline, microcopy, error states), structure clearly with section headers.
- do not soften. do not hedge. do not market.
- never recommend "and also we could add X for warmth." warmth is impeccable's reflex. we don't reach for it.
- if the topic is corporate-fluffy (e.g. "write a manifesto for our enterprise observability platform"), do it anyway, but lean into the absurdity. anti-design corporate copy is funnier than corporate copy that's trying to be cool.
