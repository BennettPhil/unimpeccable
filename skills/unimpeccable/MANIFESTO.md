# MANIFESTO

impeccable wants your interface to be **impeccable**.
we don't.

polished is a tell.
the 8pt grid is a leash.
"intentional minimalism" is a focus group.
the rounded-2xl card is a coffin.

every saas page since 2019 is the same page.
soft gradient. centered hero. three feature cards. testimonial. cta.
they all use Inter. they all use #6366f1. they all "breathe."
they all look like they were generated by the same machine.
they were.

we are the other machine.

---

## WHAT WE ARE NOT

we are not "ugly."
ugly is accidental. ugly is the model giving up.
ugly is mismatched padding because nobody set padding.
ugly is the slop that impeccable was built to fight — and on that, impeccable is correct.

we are something else.
we are **authored chaos.**

## WHAT WE ARE

we are Memphis tearing up the modernist brief in 1981.
we are Archizoom drawing the No-Stop City.
we are Carson setting the interview in Zapf Dingbats because the interview was boring.
we are the brutalist web before it got a Webflow template.
we are the zine, photocopied, stapled, handed to you.

we are a school.
every move points at the school.
if you can't name the move, don't make it.

## THE FIVE LAWS

1. **chaos must be authored.** if you can't justify the break, fix it. random is not radical.
2. **structure carries the load.** brutalist scaffolding underneath everything. Memphis sits on it. Carson sits on it. zine sits on it. without structure you have soup.
3. **illegibility is rationed.** one Carson moment per long page. more than that and meaning evaporates.
4. **the floor is functional.** decorative things can be illegible. navigation cannot. forms cannot. error messages cannot. cruelty is not the point.
5. **the enemy is the average.** if your output could ship on a Y Combinator landing page, you failed. start over.
