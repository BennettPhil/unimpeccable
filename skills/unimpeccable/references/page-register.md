# page-register

every unimpeccable page exists in one of three registers. the register determines how rations scale and which compositional rules apply. ask the user. or infer from the brief. lock it before building.

## REGISTER 1 — PRODUCT

a single anti-design page selling, explaining, or operating one thing. saas pages explicitly framed as anti-design. portfolio sites. product feature pages. login screens. dashboards re-imagined.

rules.

- ONE school per page. lock it. no school-mixing.
- rations are conservative — the page is the unit:
  - 2 to 3 memphis motifs per section
  - one carson moment per page
  - one signature motion per page
- the standard four authorized grid breaks apply (asymmetric split, full-bleed overlap, diagonal flow, negative indent). page-level breaks are NOT used here.

this is the default register for anything that isn't obviously showcase or artifact.

## REGISTER 2 — SHOWCASE / MANIFESTO

a long-scroll page presenting multiple schools, the skill itself, a manifesto, a survey, or any meta-content about anti-design. anything containing multiple distinct content areas that warrant different visual treatments.

rules.

- MULTIPLE schools allowed. encouraged.
- the **school-at-section** rule applies: each major section adopts its content's school as its entire compositional grammar. not styling-inside-the-section. the section IS the school.
- rations scale per section, not per page:
  - 3 to 5 memphis motifs per memphis section
  - one carson moment per ~1000px of scroll
  - one signature motion per major section
  - one scroll-triggered follower per section
- the three page-level breaks apply (section bleed, viewport extension, scale variation across siblings) in addition to the standard four.
- sibling items in a row (cards, blocks) MUST NOT share dimensions. each footprint matches its content's character.
- sections must crash into each other. section bleed is mandatory at major boundaries.

the unit is the SECTION. think of the page as a chord of schools, not a chord of content variations.

## REGISTER 3 — ARTIFACT

a period piece. a 1999 frutiger-aero homepage. a 1994 ray gun spread. a 1992 zine. a single school taken seriously as the entire register, often with historical fidelity to the year.

rules.

- ONE school per page. extreme commitment.
- rations from the school apply, but every decision tilts toward "more, not less".
- the entire visual register matches the artifact's era. structure, type, color, motion, voice — all from the same year.
- a y2k artifact has NO memphis motifs. a 1992 zine has NO chrome gradients. cross-pollination breaks the artifact.

artifact register is the hardest to do well. ask the user to name the specific year and the specific reference (the artifact you're channeling). if they can't, recommend showcase or product register instead.

## WHICH REGISTER?

ask the user. infer if they don't say. defaults.

- "build a page for X product / brand / service" → product
- "build a manifesto / showcase / promo / about-page for [the thing itself]" → showcase
- "build a [year] / [historical reference] site for X" → artifact
- "anti-design the homepage of [my saas]" → product
- "build a site that shows off all eight schools" → showcase

if the brief is ambiguous, ASK before locking. wrong-register pages are the most common cause of timidity failure. a manifesto built in product register reads as one bland page with anti-design styling. a product built in showcase register reads as a confused survey.

## SCALING RULES (QUICK REFERENCE)

| ration | product | showcase | artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| schools per page | 1 | 1 per major section | 1 |
| memphis motifs per section | 2-3 | 3-5 | from school |
| carson moments | 1 per page | 1 per ~1000px scroll | from school |
| signature motion | 1 per page | 1 per major section | from school |
| section bleed | none | 1-2 per page | none |
| viewport extension | none | 1-2 per page | none |
| scale variation across siblings | optional | MANDATORY | optional |

rations are FLOORS, not ceilings. when in doubt, push further. timidity fails.
